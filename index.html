Sharing with a partner or caregiver

Open Settings → Family → Invite and send the invite by Messages or Mail. When they open it on their iPhone, your family's children appear in their app and everyone can log meals. Both people need to be signed in to iCloud.

My data isn't showing up on another phone

Make sure both phones are signed in to iCloud, that iCloud is allowed for First Bites (iOS Settings → your name → iCloud), and that both have a network connection. Changes usually sync within a minute.

Adding foods your baby already ate

Use Foods → Already tried (the checklist button at the top) or Settings → Foods tried before the app. Allergens marked there count as introduced.

Breast milk, formula or both

Each child has a Drinks setting in Settings → Milk and nutrition. It sets sensible defaults for the vitamin D reminder and the iron target, and every value can be adjusted.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings → Your data to delete one child or everything you created. This removes it from your iPhone and from iCloud.

Medical note

First Bites offers general information, not medical advice. Targets and suggestions are based on published guidance and may not suit every child. Talk with your pediatrician about your baby's needs, especially around allergies, iron and supplements. If your baby has trouble breathing, swelling of the face or lips, or widespread hives after eating, call 911.
