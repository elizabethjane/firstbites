Privacy policy

First Bites · Effective October 20, 2026

HomeSupportPrivacy

Short version: we don't collect anything. What you enter stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account, shared only with people you invite.

What the app stores

The information you enter: your children's names, optional birthdays and photos, meals, foods, recipes, reactions, notes and settings.

Where it's stored

On your iPhone, and, if you're signed in to iCloud, in your private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit so it syncs across your devices. If you invite someone to your family, the family's data is shared with them through iCloud sharing. The developer of First Bites cannot see, access or recover this data. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

What we collect

Nothing. First Bites has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. We don't sell or share data because we never receive it.

Photos

If you add a photo for a child, you choose it with the iOS photo picker. Only that photo is used, and it's stored with your data as described above. The app does not access the rest of your library.

Links

Food pages link to Solid Starts (solidstarts.com), which opens in your browser and is covered by that site's own privacy policy.

Children

First Bites is designed for parents and caregivers to use, not for children. Information about a child is entered by their parent or caregiver and stays in that family's own storage.

Deleting data

Delete a child or all of your data in Settings → Your data. You can also remove app data from iCloud in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage. Deleting the app removes data stored on that iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
